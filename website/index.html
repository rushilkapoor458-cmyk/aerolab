Our story

A kitchen that only knows one heat source.

Aurelia began as an argument. Ravi Menon had spent six years in a two-star kitchen in Copenhagen learning to control temperature to the degree, and came home convinced that the control was the problem — that the food he actually remembered had all been cooked badly, over fire, by someone's grandmother.

So there is no induction on our line. Wood, coal and clay, which are slower, harder to hold steady, and the only way we know to get this flavour. The menu is written after the morning delivery arrives rather than before, which means it is never quite the same twice.
